How scoring works

Your in-game score increases as you reach higher platforms. Your best unsaved result is used for the Zat.am leaderboard, capped at 100 points.

Run time is recorded but does not affect your score. Nothing is submitted until you select Save best score.

Platform guide

Normal: safe landing and can restore life.

Spring: launches the character upward.

Conveyor: pushes the character sideways.

Fake: disappears after you stand on it.

Hazard: damages your life when you land.
