Help

    How to play

    1. Find the Sanskrit words from the Bhagavad Gita shloka
    2. Tap the first अक्षर (character) of each word.
    3. Words can appear horizontally, vertically, or diagonally in any direction. Found words are highlighted.
    4. Incorrect clicks cost 1 point. Correct clicks count towards the Words Found counter.
    5. Hint 1 shows the full shloka in order (−10 points). Hint 2 then unlocks and bolds the hidden words (−20 points). Each hint costs points once.
    6. Choose a difficulty to play a new board, or select Retry to try again.
    7. Final scores are submitted to the leaderboard
