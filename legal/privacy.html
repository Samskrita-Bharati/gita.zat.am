Tracking Technologies and Cookies

We use Cookies and similar tracking technologies to support certain features of Our Service and to understand website activity.

  • Cookies or Browser Cookies. A cookie is a small file placed on Your Device. You can instruct Your browser to refuse all Cookies or to indicate when a Cookie is being sent. However, if You do not accept Cookies, You may not be able to use some parts of our Service.
  • Web Beacons. Certain sections of our Service and our emails may contain small electronic files known as web beacons (also referred to as clear gifs, pixel tags, and single-pixel gifs) that permit the Company to count users who have visited those pages or opened an email and for other related website statistics.

Cookies can be "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies. Persistent Cookies remain on Your personal computer or mobile device when You go offline, while Session Cookies are deleted as soon as You close Your web browser.

Authentication and other essential technologies may be used to provide account-related functionality. Analytics technologies may also be used when enabled on the Website. You can manage cookies through Your browser or device settings. For questions about analytics tracking, consent, or available privacy choices, please contact Us using the information provided below.

Firebase Authentication and User Accounts

We use Google Firebase Authentication to manage user registration, sign-in, and account security. Users may create an Account using an email address and password or sign in using a supported Google account.

Depending on Your sign-in method, Firebase Authentication may process information such as Your email address, display name, authentication provider, and a unique user identifier.

We use Google Cloud Firestore to store information associated with registered Accounts. This may include profile information, selected preferences, account creation dates, recent login timestamps, and login streak information.

We use this information to provide personalized account features, maintain user preferences, manage access to the Service, and support the functionality of Our educational games.

Game Activity, Scores, and Learning Progress

When You use games on Zat.am, We may collect and store information about Your game activity. Depending on the game and its supported features, this information may include game identifiers, game names, level information, scores, results, elapsed time, completion timestamps, and recorded attempts.

We use this information to provide leaderboards, display achievements, maintain game-related records, and help improve Our educational games.

Certain games may also save learning progress, such as completed shlokas, to help maintain progress-related features.

Some leaderboard information, including display names and recorded scores, may be visible to other signed-in users of the Service.

Game Page Visits and Activity Tracking

We use a game-visit tracking feature to understand which educational games are visited and how frequently they are accessed.

For eligible signed-in users visiting supported game pages, We may record a Firebase user identifier, game identifier, game name, page path, and visit timestamp in Cloud Firestore.

This information helps Us understand game popularity, monitor participation, and improve the learning experience. Visit records may be subject to limits intended to avoid counting repeated page refreshes as separate visits.

A recorded game page visit does not necessarily mean that the game was started, completed, or scored.

Administrator Dashboard and Reporting

Zat.am provides a restricted administration dashboard for authorized administrators.

Administrators may access reports based on information stored in Cloud Firestore, including registered profiles, registration activity, login activity, recorded streaks, game page visits, leaderboard records, and game performance statistics.

Administrative access is controlled through account permissions and database security rules. These reports are used to understand how the Service is used, support its operation, and identify areas for improvement.

Reports generated from Firestore are separate from Google Analytics website traffic reports. Because they use different collection methods, their statistics may not always match.

Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4, a web analytics service provided by Google, to understand how visitors interact with Our Website.

Google Analytics may collect information about page views, website interactions, approximate location, browser and device characteristics, and other website usage information.

Google Analytics may use cookies and similar technologies to measure website activity. Analytics information is used to understand traffic patterns, identify popular content, evaluate website performance, and improve the Service.

Information collected through Google Analytics is processed according to Google's applicable terms and privacy practices. For additional information, please review Google's Privacy Policy and Google Analytics Data Safeguards.
